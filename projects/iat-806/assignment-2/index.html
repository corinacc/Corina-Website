Assignment 2 · Body Game

Notes

Have fun playing the game!

How to play

  1. Press SPACE to start.
  2. Match your body to the dark grey shape of the person in the painting.
  3. Reach the match score before the time run out
  4. Watch out for bombs falling down. If one hits the red dot on your nose, you lose 5 seconds.
  5. Clear all three paintings to win!
