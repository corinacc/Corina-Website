Dance Animation

Lab 03 · A six-frame flipbook animation in p5.js.

Notes

Watch the robot dance!

  • Click the canvas — plays the next sound effect (3) and randomizes the background color.
  • Space — pauses the dance animation; press again to resume.
  • S — makes every dancer twice as fast; press again to go back to normal speed.
  • N — adds another dancer to the right, up to 5 total.
  • 1 — bonus: freezes only the first robot; everyone else keeps dancing. Press again to let it rejoin.
