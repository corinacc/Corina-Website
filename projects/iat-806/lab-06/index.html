Lab 06 · Body balls

Notes

What I did

  • Step 1: a Ball class. 1500 purple balls shake and change size every frame.
  • Step 2: respond(active) turns a ball yellow or random colour with super ball, or back to purple.
  • Step 3: click a ball and it turns yellow.
  • Step 4: ml5.js bodySegmentation finds my body in the webcam. isOnBody() checks the mask under each ball, so balls on my body turn yellow.

Bonus

  • Click and body together: a ball is active if it was clicked or it is on my body.
  • Active balls shake harder: they move and grow 4× more than normal balls.
  • Super Ball: extends Ball. It starts purple. When it is clicked (active), it flashes random colors and shakes harder.
