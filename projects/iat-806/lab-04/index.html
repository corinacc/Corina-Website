The Dance Floor

Lab 04 · A grid of dancers that react to the mouse in p5.js.

Notes

Move the mouse across the dance floor and watch the dancers react!

  • Move the mouse left and right — changes how many columns of dancers are on the floor.
  • Dancers close to the mouse dance faster, get bigger, and turn into a dancing piece of bread; dancers far away slow down, shrink, and fade.
  • Everywhere else, the floor is a checkerboard of the dance and the jump.

What I added

  • Speed from the mouse: each dancer finds the middle of its cell and uses dist() to see how far it is from the mouse. map() turns that distance into a number from 0.5 (close) to 3 (far), and the dancer’s own random speed is multiplied by it. Close dancers go faster, far ones go slower, and they still don’t all move together.
  • Two dances: the 4 jump poses are loaded into frames2. Cells where (i + j) is even do the dance and odd cells do the jump, which makes a checkerboard. Both use the same animate() function with a different array.
  • Bug fix: getFrameIndex() used to always count with the 8 dance poses, so the 4-pose jump froze after a moment. Now the poses are passed in, so each dance loops through its own number of poses.

Bonus

  • Size: dancers are 1.5 times the row height next to the mouse and half the row height far away. I finished the TODO in animate(): if only the height is given, the width is worked out with the same scale, so the dancers never get squished. imageMode(CENTER) makes them grow from the middle of their cell.
  • See-through: tint() fades the dancers the farther they are from the mouse, from fully solid to almost invisible. noTint() turns it off after each dancer.
  • A third dance, picked by the mouse: any dancer less than 150 pixels from the mouse turns into a dancing piece of bread (6 poses). The bread has its own size, from 1.6 times the row height right under the mouse to 0.6 at the edge of the circle.
