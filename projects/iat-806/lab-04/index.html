The Dance Floor

Lab 04 · A grid of dancers that react to the mouse in p5.js.

Notes

Move the mouse across the dance floor and watch the dancers react!

  • Move the mouse left and right — changes how many columns of dancers are on the floor.
  • Dancers close to the mouse dance faster, get bigger, and turn into a dancing piece of bread; dancers far away slow down, shrink, and fade.

What I added

  • Speed from the mouse: dist() measures how far each dancer is from the mouse. map() uses that distance to change the dancer’s speed: close dancers go faster, far ones slower.
  • New dancers: I added a 4-frame jump dancer and a 6-frame bread.
  • Bug fix: getFrameIndex() always used the global 8-pose frames, so I added a frames to getFrameIndex(frames, speed), so each dancer loops through its own poses. I also check that the mouse is inside the canvas.

Bonus

  • Distance for more than speed: the distance to the mouse sets each dancer’s speed, size, and transparency. Close dancers are faster, bigger, and solid; far ones are slower, smaller, and faded.
  • Distance picks the dance: dancers within 150 pixels of the mouse turn into bread; the rest keep the dance/jump checkerboard.
  • A third dance: a 6-frame dancing bread.
