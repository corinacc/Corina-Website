Riddle of the Sphinx

Assignment 1 · An interactive puzzle in p5.js.

Notes

Truth does not reveal itself to the eyes, but to the awakened mind. Pierce the riddle, trace the letters, and let wisdom speak.

How to play

  1. Click the start screen to begin.
  2. Read the riddle and think of the answer. It is one word.
  3. Click the letters that spell the answer. A letter lights up when you click it. Click it again to turn it off.
  4. Light up only the letters of the answer. If a wrong letter is lit, the riddle won’t be solved until you turn it off.
  5. Hope you can survive!
  6. Press R to shuffle the letters and play again.
