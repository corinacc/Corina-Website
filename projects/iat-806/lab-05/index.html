Lab 05

Lab 05 · Crazy ball

Notes

What I added

  • runFromMouse(byHowMuch): when the mouse is closer than 80 pixels, the ball is pushed away from it. The push depends on how far the ball is from the mouse，so a ball farther from the center gets pushed harder.
  • glowNearMouse(range): when the mouse is closer than range pixels, the ball changes color, and it goes back to its own color when the mouse leaves. this.startColor remembers the color it started with.
  • Both methods live inside the Ball class and are called from change() before show(), so they run for every ball, every frame.

Bonus

  • A new glow color every 10 seconds: every 600 frames, draw() picks a random glowColor, and all the balls near the mouse use it.
  • Click to leave a cat: mousePressed() saves each click in catX and catY. The cats are drawn before the balls, so the balls move over them.
